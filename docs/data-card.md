Raw files are in `data/raw/` with provenance (source URL, resolved URL, download date, size, SHA-256) in `data/raw/manifest.json`.
They come from the Publications Office's Cellar endpoint (`https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>`, content-negotiated to XHTML per language), not from the eur-lex.europa.eu website: EUR-Lex answers scripted requests with a bot challenge (HTTP 202, empty body, `x-amzn-waf-action: challenge`). Both are operated by the Publications Office of the EU.

The files are the Official Journal texts as published (their `<title>` is the OJ L-series file name), not a consolidated version. Later corrigenda or amendments are therefore not reflected. This was not compared against EUR-Lex's current text.

## Corpus format

`data/processed/corpus.jsonl`, one unit per line, UTF-8. Fields: `id`, `regulation`, `language`, `type`, `article`, `annex`, `number`, `title`, `chapter`, `text`, `refs`, `parent_id`.

- `id` is language-independent (`aiact:art:6:2`, `gdpr:def:1`, `aiact:anx:III:1`), so the same id appears once per language; it is unique per (regulation, language). `parent_id` gives the hierarchy.
- Unit types: `recital`, `article`, `paragraph`, `point`, `definition`, `annex`, `annex_section`.
- Units overlap on purpose: an `article` holds its full text, its `paragraph` units hold their points inline, and each `point` is also its own unit. The definitions article (AI Act Art. 3, GDPR Art. 4) is split into `definition` units (`title` is the defined term) and gets no `paragraph`/`point` units. Do not sum counts across types to get a "size of the law".
- `chapter` is the chapter heading in the unit's language. Sections inside chapters are not recorded.

## Known parsing limitations

- **Not ingested:** the preamble's "Having regard to" citations, the final formula and signatures, and all footnote texts (footnote markers are removed from the text).
- **Unnumbered articles:** 19 AI Act and 17 GDPR articles have no numbered paragraphs in the source. They get an `article` unit, plus `point` units when they contain a lettered list (e.g. `aiact:art:16:a`), but no `paragraph` units. This includes Articles 102 to 110 of the AI Act, which quote unnumbered amendment text.
- **Repeated lists:** when one paragraph contains two lists with the same labels, the later list's points get an `lN:` prefix. This occurs once: `aiact:art:43:1:l2:a` to `l2:d`.
- **Unlabelled bullets:** list items marked only with a dash get no `point` unit; their text stays in the parent.
- **Annex sections are heuristic.** They are split on "Section A" style headings, numbered headings/items and, if there are none, lettered items. Annex II has no `annex_section` units (its items carry only a dash). Annex VIII ids include the section letter (`aiact:anx:VIII:A:1`). Annex units always hold the full annex text.
- **Definition titles** are the first quoted term of the definition. No definition fell back to the truncated-text title in any of the three languages.
- **Cross-references** (`refs`) come from `copilot.ingest.refs.extract_cross_refs` (English, French and German; lists, ranges, and paragraph levels such as `art:6:2`). Measured on the corpus: units with at least one reference are 773 (EN), 777 (FR) and 743 (DE) of 2375 per language, with 1972, 2000 and 1894 references in total. For the same unit id, EN and FR give identical reference sets on 2330 of 2375 ids and EN and DE on 2250; the remaining differences were not investigated. References to other acts ("Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679", "Article 16 TFEU") are skipped, and the target regulation of a skipped reference is not recorded. Sub-paragraph and point levels ("first subparagraph, point (g)") are not captured.
- The source contains a stray backtick in at least one title ("Subject matter`"); trailing backticks are stripped from titles. Other typos in the source are kept as published.
